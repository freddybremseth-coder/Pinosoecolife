# Pinoso Eco Life — Brand & Content Plan 1.1

Status: LOCKED
Original lock: 2026-09-16
Updated: 2026-09-17

## 1. Core positioning

Pinoso Eco Life is not primarily a property catalogue. It is a lifestyle brand for people who want to use space, land, calm and inland Spain to create a different everyday life.

### Locked brand promise

**Ikke bare et nytt hjem. En annen måte å leve på.**

Supporting message:

**Mer plass. Mer ro. Mer av livet ute.**

Signature editorial idea:

**10 000 m² er ikke bare tomt. Det er 10 000 m² med muligheter.**

The customer journey remains practical and grounded:

**Livsstil → område → egnet tomt → boligmodell → trygg gjennomføring.**

## 2. What the brand sells emotionally

The brand should help visitors imagine:

- a slower and less hectic everyday rhythm
- privacy and silence around the home
- more time outdoors
- walking and cycling from the doorstep
- a kitchen garden and fruit trees
- fresh eggs from a small flock where local rules allow it
- olives, almonds, grapes and other crops suited to the specific plot
- outdoor cooking, family visits and room for hobbies
- knowing neighbours, local shops and village life over time
- creating a place gradually instead of buying only a finished house

We do **not** promise full self-sufficiency, guaranteed buildability, agricultural permissions or a specific use of a plot. Every plot is assessed separately for planning, water, access, services and local rules.

## 3. Brand pillars

### A. Et annet tempo
Everyday life, seasons, markets, long meals, less rush and a more deliberate rhythm.

### B. Plass til å skape
Land is not empty space. It can become garden, orchard, shade, terraces, hobbies, nature and family space.

### C. Mer selvberget
A practical, realistic path to growing some of your own food, fruit and herbs and potentially keeping animals where permitted.

### D. Nærhet mellom mennesker
Smaller communities, familiar faces, local tradespeople, cafés, markets and village traditions — without romanticising or claiming that every buyer will have the same experience.

### E. Aktivt hverdagsliv
Walking, road cycling, gravel, mountain routes, gardening and outdoor work as part of normal life.

### F. Tomten som en del av hjemmet
The plot is not a leftover around the villa. It is part of the purchase decision and part of the life the customer is buying.

## 4. Commercial goal

### Primary 12-month goal

Generate enough qualified inland demand to support **4 completed Pinoso Eco Life sales per year**, with plot + new-build and suitable inland homes as the core opportunities.

### Supporting funnel targets

- 5–8 qualified inland leads per month
- 2+ advisory calls per month from lifestyle/content traffic
- 1+ serious viewing/plot visit per month
- 2 substantial editorial articles per month after launch phase
- every major article must have a relevant next step: area, plots, properties or contact

These are operating targets, not public claims.

## 5. Editorial strategy

The magazine should be split mentally into two tracks:

1. **Drømmen / Eco Life** — why someone would want this life
2. **Trygg gjennomføring** — plots, planning, utilities, buying process and due diligence

The first track creates desire. The second makes the decision credible.

## 7. Content rules

- Sell the lifestyle without pretending inland life is perfect for everyone.
- Avoid generic claims such as “cheaper”, “best”, “most authentic” or “better investment” unless supported with current data.
- Do not imply that a 10 000 m² plot is automatically buildable.
- Separate inspirational ideas from legal/planning facts.
- “Mer selvberget” is preferred over promising “selvforsynt”.
- Animal keeping, agricultural projects, extra buildings, guest houses, water use and wine production must always be described as subject to the specific property and applicable rules.
- Articles should read like useful editorial stories, not broker advertisements.
- Every article should make the reader imagine a normal weekday, not only a holiday.

## 8. Brand distinction

**Zen Eco Homes:** modern homes, projects, property advice and execution across a wider geography.

**Pinoso Eco Life:** the emotional and practical story of inland living — land, space, calm, self-directed outdoor life and the possibilities around the home.

Pinoso Eco Life should own the question:

**Hva ville du gjort hvis du hadde 10 000 m² rundt hjemmet ditt?**

## 9. Locked next phase — Design System 2027–2030

This phase starts only after the final content and factual audit is merged and production-verified.

The design goal is not a cosmetic refresh. Pinoso Eco Life should become a premium, contemporary lifestyle/property experience that feels credible for 2027–2030 while remaining calm, human and easy to use.

### Design review scope

- typography and font system across desktop and mobile
- spacing, rhythm, grid and maximum reading widths
- modern editorial hierarchy for long-form stories
- image art direction and aspect-ratio system
- hero treatments, cards and section transitions
- navigation and mobile menu
- area pages, magazine cards, property cards and plot cards
- forms, contact CTAs, chatbot and customer portal
- micro-interactions and restrained motion where it adds clarity
- accessibility, contrast, readable type sizes and touch targets
- responsive QA at phone, tablet, laptop and large desktop widths
- loading/performance so visual ambition does not make the site slow
- consistent component tokens for radius, shadows, borders, surfaces and states

### Locked design principles

1. **Editorial before portal-like.** The public website should feel like a premium lifestyle publication with property capability, not an admin dashboard.
2. **Warm modernity.** Use land, stone, wine, olive, light and natural materials as inspiration without becoming rustic or old-fashioned.
3. **Space is part of the brand.** Generous whitespace and strong photography should communicate the same freedom as a large plot.
4. **Fewer, stronger moments.** Avoid visual noise, excessive cards, unnecessary gradients and decorative effects with no purpose.
5. **Typography carries the premium feel.** Headlines, reading text, labels and numerical/property data must each have a clear role.
6. **Mobile is a first-class experience.** Long words, headings, maps, filters and forms must never collide or overflow.
7. **Facts remain visually distinct from inspiration.** Legal, planning, cost and buildability caveats must be easy to understand without destroying the emotional story.
8. **No redesign by accident before the audit is complete.** Content structure and factual correctness are frozen first; visual overhaul follows as a dedicated phase.

### Execution order after content sign-off

**Design audit → visual direction → design tokens → global shell → homepage → Eco Life pillar/areas → magazine → plots/properties → forms/chatbot/portal → responsive/accessibility/performance QA → production rollout.**

Broad email nurturing starts only after this design phase is coherent enough that email traffic lands on pages that represent the finished brand.
